= Information Presentation & Metric Tracking Expectations =
(For Future AI Context & Architecture Scaling)

## Context
Mario Kart lobbies exist in 12-Player and 24-Player configurations. 
After a race concludes, the game surfaces metrics on two distinct scoreboard screens.

## Screen 3a: Race Placement (`+Gain Prior`)
This screen appears first and sorts the players purely by how they placed in the most recent single race.
**Visual Structure:** 
- The text is aligned on the right side of the screen next to the player's name.
- It contains TWO separate number clumps: `[+X]    [Y]`.
- Output: `+Gain    Prior Score`. 
- **Example:** `RunnerXxX   +15   42`. 
- **Requirement for later:** The total running score is calculated by parsing both numbers and mathematically summing them (`42 + 15 = 57`), OR tracking the animation if it counts up. 
- **12-Player constraint:** All 12 players fit on the screen.
- **24-Player constraint:** The UI scrolls dynamically to center on the active player. If the player is in the top 13, it will only show the top 13 players.

## Screen 3b: Overall Sum Placement
This screen appears second and sorts the players by their total accumulated points over the entire set of races (the Mogi).
**Visual Structure:**
- The text is cleanly aligned on the far right.
- It contains ONE single number clump for the absolute total score.
- **Example:** `RunnerXxX     57`.
- **Requirement:** This is incredibly reliable for Computer Vision because a Regex end-of-string search (`\s(\d{1,3})$`) securely isolates the integer.

## Future Dynamic Target Detection
Currently, the OCR engine relies on a hardcoded "Alias" string (e.g. searching specifically for the literal characters "RunnerXxX"). 
In the future, the backend should be rewritten to detect the Player automatically. Since the active host player is always **highlighted** in the UI with a distinct yellow/bright rectangular background on the scoreboard, OpenCV or simple Image Thresholding can dynamically find the Y-Coordinate of that highlighted box, crop exactly that horizontal row, and extract the Points regardless of the player's name.
